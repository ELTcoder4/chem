# AtomLab implementation outcomes

- Interactive Rutherford atom simulation showing a nucleus and orbiting electrons in a responsive visual workspace; controls pause/resume, simulation speed, and label visibility; live nucleus and particle information includes proton, neutron, and electron counts.
- Searchable and selectable molecule list for quickly finding and opening chemical compounds.
- Clear 3D-style molecular structure view with visually distinct atoms and bonds; selectable atoms and bonds have contextual highlighting and a synchronized details panel.
- Molecule overview shows formula, molecular geometry, polarity, hybridization, and useful chemistry facts.
- Structural measurements display relevant bond lengths and bond angles.
- Electron-domain information includes bonding domains, lone pairs, and geometry relationships.
- Educational, polished interface adapts across desktop and mobile and remains fully usable without login.
